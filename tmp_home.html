Department of Tourism — Philippines

The Department of Tourism (DOT) of the Republic of the Philippines is responsible for the promotion, development, and regulation of the tourism industry in the country. DOT works with local government units, industry partners, and communities to promote sustainable, inclusive, and culturally respectful tourism across the Philippines.

Mission

To develop, promote and regulate a sustainable tourism industry that will generate jobs and economic growth while preserving the environment and cultural heritage of the Philippines.

Key Programs

  • Tourism promotion and international marketing
  • Community-based and sustainable tourism development
  • Capacity building for tourism stakeholders
  • Quality assurance and safety standards

Contact

Department of Tourism, Republic of the Philippines — info@tourism.gov.ph
