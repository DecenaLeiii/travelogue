## 📖 How to Use

### For Admins
1. Navigate to `/admin/featured-categories`
2. View list of featured categories
3. Click "View Items" on any category
4. See all items with full details
5. Review and verify content
6. Make curation decisions
7. Click "Save Featured Categories"

### For Users
- Visit homepage
- See "Discover Destination Experiences" section
- See featured categories with items
- Click items to view destination details
